Add Checkbox Group (`menhir/checkbox-group`): checkboxes that hold one list of values, with `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange`, submitted as one entry per checked box under the group's `name`. A `<fieldset>` named by its `<legend>`, `required` meaning at least one box, `disabled` for the group or one box, and an optional `Parent` checkbox that checks all, none, or shows that some are checked. It joins a Field as `Field.Control`'s `render`.
